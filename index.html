Our story

Every Indian household has that one aunty who swears by her own oil.

Mom's Oil started the same way — in a kitchen, not a lab. Our founder, Madhuri Sharma, had been hand-pressing hibiscus, curry leaf and methi into coconut oil for family and neighbours for over a decade, long before it had a name or a label.

We haven't changed the recipe. We've only made it possible for you to order it, wherever you are — still small-batch, still homemade, still without a single chemical or preservative near it.

— Madhuri Sharma, Founder, Mom's Oil

Aloe Vera

Soothes the scalp and locks in moisture without leaving hair greasy.

Hibiscus

Frequently asked questions

Is this oil suitable for all hair types?

Yes — it's a light, herb-infused coconut oil base suitable for oily, dry and normal scalps. If you have a specific scalp condition, we'd recommend checking with a dermatologist first.

How often should I use it?

Most people massage it in 2–3 times a week, at least an hour before a wash, or overnight for a deeper treatment.
