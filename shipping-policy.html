Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a starting template. Update the dispatch time, delivery areas, and charges below to match how Mom's Oil actually ships once you've finalised a courier partner — then remove this note.

Order processing

Orders are confirmed once payment is received via UPI and your address is shared with us on WhatsApp. Each bottle is hand-bottled to order, so please allow 1–2 business days for your order to be prepared before it is dispatched.

Delivery timelines

Once dispatched, orders are typically delivered within:

  • Varanasi & nearby areas (Uttar Pradesh): 2–4 business days
  • Rest of India: 4–7 business days
  • Remote or ODA pincodes: up to 10 business days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to courier delays, weather, or local restrictions beyond our control.

Shipping charges

Shipping charges, if any, are shared with you on WhatsApp at the time your order is confirmed, based on your delivery pincode and order quantity.

Order tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we will share tracking details (if available from our courier partner) on the same WhatsApp number used to place the order.

Delayed or missing orders

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated window, message us on WhatsApp with your name and order date and we'll look into it with our courier partner.

Contact us

For any shipping questions, reach us on WhatsApp at +91 88879 35854 or email themomsoil@gmail.com.
